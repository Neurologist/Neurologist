### `> whoami`

Building at the intersection of **low-level systems** and **machine intelligence**. I work across the full stack — from FPGA/DMA hardware interfaces to transformer architectures and diffusion pipelines. Most of my work lives in C++, Python, and Rust.

Currently focused on:
- 🧠 **LLM inference optimization** — vLLM, llama.cpp, custom CUDA kernels
- 🎨 **Generative AI** — Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI pipelines, LoRA training
- 🔧 **Systems programming** — DMA, memory-mapped I/O, kernel-level tooling
- 🤖 **Multi-agent architectures** — AutoGen, LangChain orchestration
- ⚡ **Edge AI** — Deploying models on constrained hardware (Pi, Jetson)
